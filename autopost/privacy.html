Privacy Policy — AutoPost Studio

Last updated: 2026-11-06

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok open_id and display name, obtained through Login Kit with the user.info.basic scope.
  • OAuth access_token and refresh_token, stored encrypted at rest and used only to call the Content Posting API on your behalf.
  • A log of publish_id values and publish status, used to show you the result of your own uploads.
  • The video file you select, held only while it is being transferred to TikTok.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your TikTok password, your followers' data, direct messages, or analytics about other users. We do not use cookies for advertising and we do not sell data.

3. How we use data

Only to authenticate you, transfer the file you selected to TikTok, and display the result of that transfer.

4. Retention

Video files are streamed to TikTok and deleted from our server within 24 hours. Tokens are kept until you disconnect. Publish logs are kept for 90 days.

5. Sharing

Data is sent to TikTok's official API only. We do not share data with advertisers or other third parties.

6. Your rights

You can revoke access in TikTok Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, or use “Disconnect” on this site. You can request deletion of all stored data by emailing the address below; deletion is completed within 7 days.
