Terms of Service — AutoPost Studio

Last updated: 2026-11-06

1. What the service does

AutoPost Studio is a web tool that lets a creator connect their own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and send video files that the creator has already produced to their own TikTok inbox as a draft, using the official Content Posting API. The service does not generate content, does not operate accounts on behalf of third parties, and does not automate engagement.

2. Your responsibilities

You must own the rights to every video you upload and comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. You are responsible for the captions, hashtags and any privacy settings you choose inside TikTok.

3. User control

The Content Posting API is called only after you explicitly click the send button for a specific file. Nothing is uploaded in the background. You may disconnect the integration at any time, from this site or from TikTok Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions.

4. Availability

The service is provided as-is. Publication may fail because of TikTok API limits, quota or policy decisions, and your TikTok account must still confirm the draft inside the TikTok app before it becomes public.

5. Termination

You may stop using the service at any time and request deletion of all stored data.
